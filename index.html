About This Project

Welcome to Anime Discovery! 🍥

Anime Discovery is a simple web app that helps users search for anime, explore, show details of that specific anime, and save their favourite anime.

✨ What You Can Do

  1. 🔎 Search Anime: Find anime by entering its name in the search bar.
  2. ⭐ Explore Ratings: Check the ratings of different anime.
  3. 📖 Read Descriptions: Discover what each anime is about using detailed synopses.
  4. ❤️ Save Your Favourites: Keep your favourite anime in one place for easy reference.
  5. 💡 Suggest Anime: Recommend anime you'd like to see featured.
